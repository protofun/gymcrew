About GymCrew

How Ranks Work

Every major lift gets its own rank — Bronze to Immortal — based on your strength relative to people at your own level, not the strongest in the world. Normalized for bodyweight and gender so it stays fair.

How Crews Work

Your Crew shares one score built from everyone's real training. Weekly Crew Challenges pit your Crew head-to-head against another crew for the win.

What Happens at Launch

Every Founding Athlete gets their badge, their Crew, and early access the moment GymCrew goes live — before it's public to anyone else.

Founding Athlete FAQ

Founding Athlete spots are limited to people who join before launch. Status is permanent once granted, as long as you stay active and keep posting.

Contact & Support

Questions before launch? Reach out any time at hello@gymcrew.site — a real person will get back to you.

⚠  Athletes Post Content

Founding Athlete status comes with an expectation: post content and stay active. Athletes who go quiet and stop posting risk losing Founding Athlete status and their spot in the program. This is for people who actually want to show up.
