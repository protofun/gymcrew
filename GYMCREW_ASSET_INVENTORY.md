## Summary guidance for the migration

- Every registry-listed asset (mascot, ranks, divisions, nutrition icons, muscle icons, nav icons, exercises) is confirmed in active use and **must be preserved and reused as-is** — these encode GymCrew's brand identity and progression systems; Reacticx supplies UI chrome/motion around them, never replacements for them.
- A meaningful number of on-disk files are **not** wired through the centralized `constants/images.ts` registry. Per this project's own CLAUDE.md convention ("do not import image assets directly inside screens or components"), any of these that *are* still referenced somewhere must be direct imports violating that rule — worth a grep-based cleanup pass during Phase 2 (Design System), not before.
- The three `react-logo*` files and `partial-react-logo.png` are near-certainly dead Expo-template leftovers — confirm and remove during cleanup, but this is routine housekeeping, not something the migration needs to design around.
